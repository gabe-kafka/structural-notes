\section{Default Drained Soil}

Preliminary values for ordinary granular retained soil. Project geotechnical recommendations govern.

{\ttfamily\footnotesize
\setlength{\arrayrulewidth}{0.5pt}
\begin{tabular*}{\textwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}}p{3.05in}r p{2.45in}@{}}
\hline
{\scriptsize\bfseries PARAMETER} & {\scriptsize\bfseries DEFAULT} & {\scriptsize\bfseries USE} \\
\hline
Effective retained-soil friction angle, $\phi'$ & $28^\circ$ & Drained strength \\
Moist total unit weight, $\gamma$ & 120--125 pcf & Retained-soil weight \\
Foundation interface friction coefficient, $\mu$ & 0.35 & Preliminary sliding resistance \\
Uniform surface surcharge, $q$ & 100 psf & Minimum general surcharge \\
Allowable soil bearing pressure, $q_a$ & 2,000 psf & Preliminary footing sizing \\
Rankine active coefficient, $K_a$ & 0.361 & Yielding wall \\
Normally consolidated at-rest coefficient, $K_0$ & 0.531 & Restrained wall \\
Active equivalent-fluid pressure & 43.3--45.1 pcf & $K_a\gamma$ \\
At-rest equivalent-fluid pressure & 63.7--66.3 pcf & $K_0\gamma$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular*}
}

Level backfill; vertical wall; no cohesion; no groundwater or hydrostatic pressure. Confirm $\mu$ and $q_a$ for the foundation soil.

\section{Default Undrained Soil}

No wall drainage is a saturated effective-stress condition. It is not automatically a $\phi_u=0$ total-stress condition.

\medskip{\ttfamily\footnotesize\bfseries SATURATED / NO-DRAIN RETAINED SOIL}

{\ttfamily\footnotesize
\setlength{\arrayrulewidth}{0.5pt}
\begin{tabular*}{\textwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}}p{3.05in}r p{2.45in}@{}}
\hline
{\scriptsize\bfseries PARAMETER} & {\scriptsize\bfseries DEFAULT} & {\scriptsize\bfseries USE} \\
\hline
Effective retained-soil friction angle, $\phi'$ & $28^\circ$ & Saturated drained strength \\
Saturated total unit weight, $\gamma_{\mathrm{sat}}$ & 125 pcf & Total soil weight \\
Fresh-water unit weight, $\gamma_w$ & 62.4 pcf & Hydrostatic pressure \\
Submerged unit weight, $\gamma'$ & 62.6 pcf & $\gamma_{\mathrm{sat}}-\gamma_w$ \\
Rankine active coefficient, $K_a$ & 0.361 & Yielding wall \\
Normally consolidated at-rest coefficient, $K_0$ & 0.531 & Restrained wall \\
Active total equivalent-fluid pressure & 85.0 pcf & $K_a\gamma'+\gamma_w$ \\
At-rest total equivalent-fluid pressure & 95.6 pcf & $K_0\gamma'+\gamma_w$ \\
Active lateral surcharge pressure & 36.1 psf & $K_aq$ \\
At-rest lateral surcharge pressure & 53.1 psf & $K_0q$ \\
Foundation interface coefficient, $\mu$ & Site-specific & Include uplift in base normal force \\
Allowable soil bearing pressure, $q_a$ & Site-specific & Saturated foundation soil \\
\hline
\end{tabular*}

Groundwater at retained grade. Effective soil pressure and full hydrostatic pressure are included in the total equivalent-fluid pressures above.

\medskip{\ttfamily\footnotesize\bfseries TRUE UNDRAINED TOTAL-STRESS SOIL}

{\ttfamily\footnotesize
\setlength{\arrayrulewidth}{0.5pt}
\begin{tabular*}{\textwidth}{@{\extracolsep{\fill}}p{3.05in}r p{2.45in}@{}}
\hline
{\scriptsize\bfseries PARAMETER} & {\scriptsize\bfseries DEFAULT} & {\scriptsize\bfseries USE} \\
\hline
Total-stress friction angle, $\phi_u$ & $0^\circ$ & Saturated undrained clay \\
Saturated total unit weight, $\gamma_{\mathrm{sat}}$ & 125 pcf & Total-stress soil weight \\
Undrained shear strength, $s_u$ & Site-specific & Required geotechnical input \\
Foundation interface adhesion, $c_a$ & Site-specific & Preliminary sliding resistance \\
Uniform surface surcharge, $q$ & 100 psf & Minimum general surcharge \\
Allowable soil bearing pressure, $q_a$ & Site-specific & Determine from foundation-soil $s_u$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular*}
}

For a yielding wall with level backfill:

\begin{equation}
p_a(z)=\max\left[0,\ \gamma_{\mathrm{sat}}z+q-2s_u\right]
\end{equation}
{\ttfamily\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
$p_a(z)$ & total active lateral pressure at depth $z$, psf \\
$z$ & depth below retained grade, ft
\end{tabular}
}

Do not derive $s_u$ from $\phi'$, $\mu$, or $q_a$. Do not add pore pressure separately throughout a consistent total-stress analysis; account separately for free water, differential water levels, uplift, and water-filled tension cracks.
