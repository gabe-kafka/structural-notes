# Structural Notes Agent Instructions

Follow `CLAUDE.md` for the repo workflow, template rules, and note style.

## Final Pass

Before handing a note back:

- Hard rule: every variable that appears in an equation must be explicitly called out in the note. Do not leave symbols implicit.
- Remove verbosity. Keep prose short, direct, and calculation-useful.
- Add only what makes the hand user's life easier: required-input lists, calculation order, dependency equations, controlling-value callouts, and concise variable legends.
- Do not add extra design background, commentary, examples, alternatives, or code discussion unless Gabe asks for them.
- Visually review every rebuilt PDF at readable zoom for consistent spacing and alignment, with zero overlapping, clipping, or obscured text, dimensions, tables, or graphics. Fix all defects before returning the PDF.
- Feet-inch dimensions in tables, equations, labels, and graphics must use both units, including zero inches: `22'-0"`. Do not use `22 ft`, `22 FT`, or `22'`.
- Stress-line graphics must break each line at its midpoint and center `S` in the gap. Never draw the line through or behind the `S`.
- Rebuild the PDF after the final pass. Do not launch macOS Preview.
