\small
\textbf{Normative basis.} ACI 318-19 for required strength and detailing;
ACI 421.1R-20 for general-shape perimeter mechanics and stud-rail guidance;
ASTM A1044/A1044M-16a for headed shear stud assemblies. This document is
version-locked. It is not an ACI 318-25 calculation.

\textbf{Scope.} Nonprestressed, solid two-way slabs without beams, reinforced
with vertical smooth headed shear studs. Inch-pound equations only. Prestressed
slabs, stirrups, shearheads, slabs with void systems, and seismic deformation
checks require separate methods.
\end{minipage}}
\end{center}

\section{Input Contract}

The calculation shall not infer structural properties from display geometry.
Every slab region shall reference one assigned slab type.

{\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{1.65in}X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Input group} & \textbf{Required data} \\
\midrule
Slab type &
$h$, $f'_c$, concrete density class or $\lambda$, top and bottom reinforcing
bar size, spacing, layer order, cover, and $f_y$ in both orthogonal directions. \\
Support &
Actual loaded/reaction footprint; support centroid $O$; interior, edge, or
corner classification; slab boundaries; thickness changes; openings. \\
Actions &
Signed $V_u$, $M_{sc,Ox}$, and $M_{sc,Oy}$ for every applicable factored load
combination, all reported at the same point $O$ and in the same axes. \\
Stud assembly &
ASTM A1044/A1044M assembly, stud shaft diameter $D$, specified design yield
strength $f_{yt}$, rail locations, stud coordinates, base-rail orientation,
head/rail covers, and assembly height. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
}

\textbf{Validation.} Require $h,d,f'_c,f_y,f_{yt}>0$,
$0.75\leq\lambda\leq1.0$, $0<\lambda_s\leq1.0$, consistent force/length units,
and stud area consistent with the certified shaft diameter. Do not accept an
independent diameter and area that disagree.

\section{Effective Depth}

For each load combination, identify the actual tension reinforcement relative
to the extreme compression face. Using the reinforcement centroids,
\begin{equation}
d=\frac{d_x+d_y}{2}.
\end{equation}
$d_x$ and $d_y$ are distances from the extreme compression face to the
centroids of the tension reinforcement in the two slab directions. Layer order
matters. For moment reversal, the bottom mat can govern and $d$ shall be
recomputed. Bar spacing does not directly enter ACI 318-19 punching strength,
but it does enter the companion flexural-reinforcement checks in Section 10.

\section{Critical Sections}

\subsection{Inner Sections}

For two-way shear, select the polygon having minimum effective perimeter $b_o$
that is not closer than $d/2$ to:
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item edges or corners of the column, concentrated load, or reaction area; and
\item changes in slab thickness, including drop panels, capitals, or shear caps.
\end{itemize}
Check every applicable section. A circular or regular polygonal support may be
replaced by a square of equal area when permitted by ACI 318-19 22.6.4.1.2.

At a slab edge, the critical section may terminate at the free edge. Do not
count a fictitious segment along the free edge.

\subsection{Openings}

If the nearest opening boundary is closer than $4h$ to the periphery of the
column, load, or reaction area, draw tangents from the support/load centroid to
the opening. The portion of each critical perimeter between those tangents is
ineffective. Apply the rule independently to the inner and outer sections.
Openings at $4h$ or farther do not reduce $b_o$ under ACI 318-19 22.6.4.3.

\subsection{Outer Section With Studs}

Also check a polygon at $d/2$ beyond the actual outermost peripheral line of
studs, selected to minimize $b_o$. Build this perimeter from the real endpoint
of every rail/face. A single maximum stud radius is not a valid substitute for
the actual layout. At free edges, terminate the effective perimeter at the slab
edge.

\section{Net Actions at Each Perimeter}

For every load combination and every critical perimeter $P$, use the net
factored actions crossing that perimeter:
\begin{equation}
V_u(P)=R_u-\int_{A_{\mathrm{inside}}(P)}q_u\,dA.
\end{equation}
Apply the same equilibrium to both moment components. If the loads inside a
perimeter are intentionally ignored, report that assumption; do not silently
reuse an inner demand at the outer perimeter.

Translate moments from support centroid $O$ to critical-section centroid $C$.
Using the ACI 421 sign convention,
\begin{equation}
M_{sc,\bar x}=M_{sc,O\bar x}+V_u\bar y_O,\qquad
M_{sc,\bar y}=M_{sc,O\bar y}+V_u\bar x_O,
\end{equation}
where $(\bar x_O,\bar y_O)$ locates $O$ from $C$. Then rotate both geometry and
moments to the centroidal principal axes:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
x&=\bar x\cos\theta+\bar y\sin\theta,&
y&=-\bar x\sin\theta+\bar y\cos\theta,\\
M_{sc,x}&=M_{sc,\bar x}\cos\theta-M_{sc,\bar y}\sin\theta,&
M_{sc,y}&=M_{sc,\bar x}\sin\theta+M_{sc,\bar y}\cos\theta.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}

\section{General-Shape Section Properties}

Partition the effective perimeter into straight segments with endpoints
$(x_i,y_i)$ and $(x_j,y_j)$ and length $L_k$. First calculate the line centroid:
\begin{equation}
b_o=\sum_k L_k,\qquad A_c=b_od,\qquad
\bar x=\frac{\sum_k L_k(x_i+x_j)/2}{b_o},\qquad
\bar y=\frac{\sum_k L_k(y_i+y_j)/2}{b_o}.
\end{equation}
Shift all endpoint coordinates to $(\bar x,\bar y)=(0,0)$. In any centroidal
axes,
\begin{align}
J_{\bar x}&=d\sum_k\frac{L_k}{3}
  \left(y_i^2+y_iy_j+y_j^2\right),\\
J_{\bar y}&=d\sum_k\frac{L_k}{3}
  \left(x_i^2+x_ix_j+x_j^2\right),\\
J_{\bar x\bar y}&=d\sum_k\frac{L_k}{6}
  \left(2x_iy_i+x_iy_j+x_jy_i+2x_jy_j\right).
\end{align}
Rotate to principal axes so $J_{xy}=0$. A nonzero $J_{xy}$ is not a warning
condition: it requires the inertia tensor and both moment components to be
transformed. The principal-axis values $J_x$ and $J_y$ shall be positive.

\section{Factored Shear-Stress Distribution}

In centroidal principal axes, with signs defined by equilibrium,
\begin{equation}
v_u(x,y)=\frac{V_u}{b_od}
 +\frac{\gamma_{vx}M_{sc,x}y}{J_x}
 +\frac{\gamma_{vy}M_{sc,y}x}{J_y}.
\end{equation}
Evaluate the signed expression at every endpoint of every effective segment for
every factored load combination:
\begin{equation}
v_{u,\max}=\max_{\mathrm{combos,\ endpoints}}\left|v_u(x,y)\right|.
\end{equation}
Because the assumed stress is linear on each straight segment, its extrema occur
at segment endpoints. The calculator shall use the conservative endpoint value;
it shall not apply the optional isolated-peak redistribution discussed in ACI
421 unless that option is separately implemented, limited, and reported.

\section{Moment Transfer Fractions}

Let $l_x$ and $l_y$ be the full projections of the current critical perimeter
onto its principal $x$ and $y$ axes. Recompute these values and the coefficients
for the outer section.

\subsection{Interior Connection}
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{vx}=1-\frac{1}{1+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{l_y/l_x}},\qquad
\gamma_{vy}=1-\frac{1}{1+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{l_x/l_y}}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Edge Connection}

For a standard three-sided perimeter, take $x$ normal to the free edge and $y$
parallel to it:
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{vx}=1-\frac{1}{1+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{l_y/l_x}},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{vy}=
\begin{cases}
0, & l_x/l_y\leq0.2,\\[2pt]
1-\dfrac{1}{1+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{l_x/l_y-0.2}}, & l_x/l_y>0.2.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Corner Connection}

For the standard two-sided perimeter, label the principal axes per ACI 421 with
$l_x\leq l_y$:
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{vx}=0.40,\qquad
\gamma_{vy}=
\begin{cases}
0, & l_x/l_y\leq0.2,\\[2pt]
1-\dfrac{1}{1+\frac{2}{3}\sqrt{l_x/l_y-0.2}}, & l_x/l_y>0.2.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}

Do not classify a perimeter from a bounding box. If openings, multiple free
edges, or a nonstandard footprint create a topology that does not match the
ACI 421 interior/edge/corner cases, require an engineer-specified, documented
method instead of inventing $\gamma_v$.

The basic ACI 318 moment split is $\gamma_v=1-\gamma_f$. Do not reduce
$\gamma_v$ under the optional ACI 318-19 8.4.2.2.4 redistribution unless its
$v_{uv}$, strain, and flexural-strength conditions are all checked and
$\gamma_fM_{sc}$ is transferred to the slab reinforcement.

\section{Concrete Strength Without Studs}

ACI 318-19 22.6.3 limits the concrete strength used to calculate $v_c$:
\begin{equation}
f'_{c,v}=\min(f'_c,10{,}000\ \mathrm{psi}).
\end{equation}
Use the specified $f'_c$ for checks that are not calculations of $v_c$, unless
their governing provision states otherwise. For nonprestressed members, in psi,
\begin{equation}
v_c=\min\!\left\{
\begin{aligned}
&4\lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_{c,v}}\\
&2\left(1+\frac{2}{\beta}\right)\lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_{c,v}}\\
&\left(2+\frac{\alpha_s d}{b_o}\right)
  \lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_{c,v}}
\end{aligned}\right\},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\lambda_s=\min\!\left(1.0,\sqrt{\frac{2}{1+d/10}}\right).
\end{equation}
$\beta$ is the long-to-short dimension ratio of the effective column, loaded,
or reaction area. For a nonrectangular area, use the ACI effective loaded-area
definition, not the critical-perimeter bounding box. Use
$\alpha_s=40,30,20$ for interior, edge, and corner connections.

\begin{equation}
\boxed{v_{u,\max}\leq\phi v_c},\qquad \phi=0.75.
\end{equation}

\section{Headed Shear Stud Strength}

\subsection{Material and Area}

Stud assemblies shall conform to ASTM A1044/A1044M. Use the specified design
yield strength certified for the assembly and accepted by the project design
basis:
\begin{equation}
f_{yt,v}=
\min(f_{yt,\mathrm{specified}},f_{yt,\mathrm{qualified}},
f_{yt,\mathrm{project\ limit}},60{,}000\ \mathrm{psi}).
\end{equation}
The $60$ ksi maximum follows ACI 318-19 22.6.3.2 and 20.2.2.4. Do not use a
higher value in $v_s$. Where the product basis specifies $51$ ksi, use
$51{,}000$ psi.

\begin{equation}
A_{\mathrm{stud}}=\frac{\pi D^2}{4},\qquad
A_v=N_pA_{\mathrm{stud}},
\end{equation}
where $N_p$ is the number of studs crossing one geometrically similar
peripheral line. $A_v$ is not the area of every stud on every rail.

\subsection{Inner Stud-Reinforced Section}

\begin{equation}
v_{c,\mathrm{stud}}=\min\!\left\{
\begin{aligned}
&3\lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_{c,v}}\\
&2\left(1+\frac{2}{\beta}\right)\lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_{c,v}}\\
&\left(2+\frac{\alpha_s d}{b_o}\right)
  \lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_{c,v}}
\end{aligned}\right\},
\qquad
v_s=\frac{A_vf_{yt,v}}{b_os}.
\end{equation}

Once studs are required, the design ratio shall satisfy both strength and the
minimum reinforcement:
\begin{equation}
\left(\frac{A_v}{s}\right)_{\mathrm{req}}=
\max\!\left[
\frac{b_o}{f_{yt,v}}
\max\!\left(\frac{v_{u,\max}}{\phi}-v_{c,\mathrm{stud}},0\right),
\frac{2\sqrt{f'_c}\,b_o}{f_{yt,v}}
\right].
\end{equation}
The provided ratio is valid only for a layout with constant radial spacing $s$
and a defined $A_v$ on each peripheral line. Variable rail spacings or missing
studs break that idealization and shall be rejected or checked line-by-line with
a separately validated method.

Both inner limits are mandatory:
\begin{equation}
\boxed{v_{u,\max}\leq\phi(v_{c,\mathrm{stud}}+v_s)},\qquad
\boxed{v_{u,\max}\leq8\phi\sqrt{f'_c}}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Outer Section}

Stud contribution is zero outside the reinforced zone:
\begin{equation}
v_{c,\mathrm{out}}=2\lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_{c,v}},\qquad
\boxed{v_{u,\max,\mathrm{out}}\leq\phi v_{c,\mathrm{out}}}.
\end{equation}
Use the outer section's own $b_o$, centroid, principal axes, $J_x$, $J_y$,
$l_x$, $l_y$, $\gamma_{vx}$, $\gamma_{vy}$, net force, and net moments.

\subsection{Size-Effect Exception}

The default is the calculated $\lambda_s$. It may be set to $1.0$ only when
smooth headed shear studs are designed and detailed per ACI 318-19 8.7.7,
the smooth shaft length of every applicable stud is at most $10$ in., and
\begin{equation}
\frac{A_v}{s}\geq\frac{2\sqrt{f'_c}\,b_o}{f_{yt,v}}.
\end{equation}
Apply the accepted value consistently to the inner and outer concrete terms.
Overall assembly height and smooth shaft length are different dimensions.

\section{Companion Flexural Checks}

Studs do not eliminate slab-column flexural-transfer requirements.

\subsection{Moment Transferred by Flexure}

In each direction, reinforcement within the effective slab width $b_{\mathrm{slab}}$
shall satisfy
\begin{equation}
\phi_f M_n\geq\gamma_f M_{sc}.
\end{equation}
$b_{\mathrm{slab}}$ is the support width plus the permitted distance on each
side under ACI 318-19 8.4.2.2.3, generally limited to $1.5h$ on each side and
further limited by slab edges or drop/cap geometry.

\subsection{Flexure-Driven Punching Minimum}

For a nonprestressed two-way slab, calculate the direct shear stress without
moment transfer:
\begin{equation}
v_{uv}=\frac{|V_u|}{b_od}.
\end{equation}
If
\begin{equation}
v_{uv}>\phi\,2\lambda_s\lambda\sqrt{f'_c},
\end{equation}
provide bonded flexural tension reinforcement over $b_{\mathrm{slab}}$:
\begin{equation}
A_{s,\min,\mathrm{FDPS}}=
\frac{5v_{uv}b_{\mathrm{slab}}b_o}{\phi\alpha_s f_y}.
\end{equation}
Check both slab directions, the actual tension face for each load combination,
bar development, and the ordinary slab minimum reinforcement. For uniform bars,
$A_{s,\mathrm{prov}}\approx A_{\mathrm{bar}}b_{\mathrm{slab}}/s_{\mathrm{bar}}$;
use actual bar coordinates when available.

\section{Deterministic Check Sequence}

{\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}p{0.28in}X@{}}
\toprule
1 & Validate assigned slab type, materials, units, reinforcement layers,
support footprint, boundaries, openings, actions, and stud certification. \\
2 & For each load combination, determine the tension face and $d_x,d_y,d$. \\
3 & Construct every inner critical section; apply free-edge and $4h$ opening
rules; calculate net actions. \\
4 & Calculate centroid and full inertia tensor; rotate geometry and actions to
principal axes; compute $\gamma_v$ from the actual perimeter topology. \\
5 & Evaluate signed stress at every segment endpoint; retain the largest
absolute value and its load combination, perimeter, segment, and coordinate. \\
6 & Check concrete-only strength. If it fails, require the stud compression cap
before designing $A_v/s$. \\
7 & Check inner stud strength, minimum $A_v/s$, product properties, height,
$s_o$, $s$, $g$, and at least two peripheral lines. \\
8 & Construct the minimum outer perimeter from real outermost studs; recompute
all geometry, actions, coefficients, and stress; check concrete-only strength. \\
9 & Check $\gamma_fM_{sc}$ flexural transfer and ACI 318-19 8.6.1.2
flexure-driven punching reinforcement. \\
10 & Pass only when every factored combination and every required inner and
outer section passes. Report all governing values and code references. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
}

\section{Required Result Record}

The calculation output shall retain, not merely display:
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item code edition; slab type ID; $h,f'_c,\lambda,d_x,d_y,d$;
\item effective perimeter segments before and after edge/opening reductions;
\item $b_o,A_c,C,J_{\bar x},J_{\bar y},J_{\bar x\bar y},\theta,J_x,J_y,l_x,l_y$;
\item net $V_u,M_{sc,x},M_{sc,y}$ and $\gamma_{vx},\gamma_{vy}$ by combination;
\item controlling endpoint and signed stress components;
\item each concrete-strength branch, $f'_{c,v}$, $\lambda_s$, $\beta$, and
$\alpha_s$;
\item $D,A_{\mathrm{stud}},N_p,A_v,s,A_v/s,f_{yt},f_{yt,v}$ and every detailing
limit;
\item inner strength, compression cap, outer strength, flexural transfer, and
flexure-driven punching checks.
\end{itemize}
