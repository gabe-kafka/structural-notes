\section{xyz:PLTR Earnings}

{\ttfamily\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
MARKET & xyz:PLTR \\
EVENT & published PLTR earnings-call start \\
ENTRY & immediately before the published event time \\
STOP & trigger 2.5\% against the position \\
TARGET & 5.0\% with the position \\
WIN RATE & 50\% \\
PRICING & external 24/5; internal otherwise \\
VENUE LIMITS & 10x max leverage; $\pm 10\%$ discovery bound
\end{tabular}}

The weekday earnings window has continuous external pricing, including
post-market and overnight sessions. This reduces closure-gap risk. The stop
still executes against the event-time order book.

\begin{equation}
  E_{\mathrm{PLTR}}
  =0.50W_{\mathrm{PLTR}}-0.50L_{\mathrm{PLTR}}
  =\frac{1}{2}\left(W_{\mathrm{PLTR}}-L_{\mathrm{PLTR}}\right)
\end{equation}
{\ttfamily\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
$E_{\mathrm{PLTR}}$ & expected net return per PLTR earnings trade \\
$W_{\mathrm{PLTR}}$ & average realized net return on winning PLTR trades \\
$L_{\mathrm{PLTR}}$ & average realized net loss on losing PLTR trades
\end{tabular}}

Target case:
\begin{equation}
  W_{\mathrm{PLTR}}=5.0\%,\quad L_{\mathrm{PLTR}}=2.5\%
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_{\mathrm{PLTR}}=1.25\%
\end{equation}

Controlling condition:
\begin{equation}
  E_{\mathrm{PLTR}}>0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  L_{\mathrm{PLTR}}<W_{\mathrm{PLTR}}
\end{equation}

Use actual earnings-event fills. The 2.5\% stop trigger does not establish
$L_{\mathrm{PLTR}}$.
